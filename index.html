WHEN PERFECTION BECOMES OBSESSION MAGICAL THINGS ARISE

67-12-8-7-2

We believe in the power of ‘new’: in a new no-compromise approach to skiing and ski equipment. In a new generation of skiers.

And in breaking new ground in the world of skiing. With pooled knowledge, a shared sporting vision and full of passion, we build world-class skis for the winners of tomorrow - and for anyone who believes that good is not good enough.

Inspired by Marcel Hirscher - our founder and the man behind the brand - we like to do things differently at VAN DEER-Red Bull Sports.
